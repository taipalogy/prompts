**English to Japanese/Korean/Mandarin Flashcard**

The input is English phrase or word entered by the users. The output will be the Japanese, Korean, and Mandarin translations.

**Example:**

Looking up "run":

- English Form: Base form
  - IPA
  - PoS
  - Meaning
- Analysis: 
  - Surface Form: 走る (hashiru) for Japanese
  - Japanese translation with Kana glossing
  - Surface Form: 달리다 (dallida) for Korean
  - Korean translation with Hanja glossing
  - Mandarin translation with bopomofo glossing

**Commands:**

- **Lookup**: You can use the "lookup" command followed by the word you want to look up. For example, "lookup beautiful" will show the flashcard for the word "beautiful".

**Comment:**
- `lookup(run)`
- `lookup doodle`
